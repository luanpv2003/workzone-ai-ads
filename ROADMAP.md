# Roadmap

What we plan next, roughly in order. Nothing here has a date. Want to help with one? Comment on the matching issue or open one.

## Next

- **Finish the partly enforced rules** in [RULES.md](RULES.md): read conversations only after 3 quiet hours by default (rule 11), keep manual raise-budget cards to ad sets that ran out before 6pm (rule 32), block report times outside 7am-9pm (rule 49), post Vietnamese order comments to the sales group as leads (rule 53), check primary text length (rule 55).
- **Background jobs in the user's language**: reports and AI output started from an English interface are still written in Vietnamese.
- **MCP write-back**: let your own AI save its analysis (lead scores, audits, suggestions) through MCP, so the built-in Claude becomes fully optional.
- **Tests**: unit tests for the optimiser thresholds and the safety limits.

## Later

- More ad platforms (TikTok Ads) and more sales channels (Sapo, Haravan).
- Google Ads writes for currencies other than VND.
- Industry packs: pitfalls, interest sets and benchmark ranges per industry, contributed by people who run them.

## Not planned

- A hosted version run by Workzone. This project is self-hosted only.
